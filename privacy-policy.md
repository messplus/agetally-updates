# AgeTally Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AgeTally is developed by Anor Group. It does not collect, store or share your personal information.

## Information we collect
We do not collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login. The dates you enter are used only on your phone to calculate the result. They are not saved and are never uploaded.

## Information stored on your device
The app saves only one setting on your phone: your chosen theme (Light, Dark or System), so it is remembered the next time you open the app. This stays on your device.

## Internet use
All calculations work offline. The app uses the internet only to check whether a newer version of AgeTally is available, when you open the app or tap "Check for updates" in Settings. It downloads a small public file that contains version information. Nothing about you, your phone or your dates is sent. As with any internet request, the server that hosts that file can see your device's IP address and the time of the request.

## Ads and tracking
- The app does not show ads.
- It does not use analytics or tracking tools.
- It does not include any third-party service that processes your data.

## Permissions
AgeTally asks only for internet access, used for the update check described above. It does not ask for contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage.

## Sharing
When you tap "Share Result", the app opens your phone's standard share menu with a short text of the result. The text goes only to the app and person you choose. AgeTally itself does not send it anywhere.

## Children
The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and on this page, with a new date.

## Contact
For any question about this policy, contact us using the support email shown on the AgeTally store page.
